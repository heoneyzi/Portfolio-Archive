03 / ABOUT

An engineering mindset.
A biological question.

I like turning biological questions into experiments we can actually test. I study how foundation models encode information, then ask whether the findings hold beyond the conditions in which they were discovered.

My work connects genomic interpretability, protein-function prediction, and cellular perturbation through PyTorch experimentation, GPU research workflows, and evaluation design. Leading teams and writing about AI have also shaped how I communicate ideas and build with others.

What I bring to a research team

  • Biological questions translated into reproducible experiments
  • Evaluation designed around generalization and leakage control
  • Experience leading four- and six-person teams and communicating technical work
